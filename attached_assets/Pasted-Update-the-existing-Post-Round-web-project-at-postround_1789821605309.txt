# 1. Privacy Policy — `/privacy`

Create a clear, professional Privacy Policy for Post Round.

The product is:

**Post Round**

Website:

`https://postroundcoach.com`

Post Round is a golf app that helps players capture their rounds, learn from their play, create player stories, receive AI-generated coaching/reflections, and optionally share golf stories/content with creators.

The policy should accurately describe the types of information the app may process, including:

### Account information

* Email address
* Account/profile information
* Subscription/entitlement information
* User preferences

### Golf and round information

* Golf rounds
* Scores and scorecard information
* Hole-by-hole information
* Player notes
* Golf performance/statistical information
* Course information
* Round history

### Voice/audio

Post Round includes an optional Round Buddy voice capture feature.

Be precise and avoid claiming that Post Round continuously records audio if that is not actually the case.

Describe voice/audio as information that may be captured when the user actively uses the applicable voice/audio functionality.

Do not make unsupported claims about how long raw audio is retained. If the current implementation does not establish a retention period, use careful language such as that audio/voice information may be processed to provide the requested functionality.

### AI processing

Explain that Post Round uses third-party AI services to process certain user-provided information in order to generate things such as:

* coaching/reflections
* player stories
* creator stories/content ideas
* summaries
* other requested AI-generated content

Do not claim that AI-generated content is always accurate.

Make clear that AI-generated coaching and insights are informational and are not a substitute for professional advice.

### Creator/social functionality

Explain that users may choose to share certain golf stories or round-related content with participating creators.

Only information the user chooses to share should be described as being shared with creators.

### Payments/subscriptions

Explain that Post Round offers paid subscriptions and/or credit purchases.

Payment processing may be handled by third-party payment providers and Apple for App Store purchases as applicable.

Do not invent specific payment providers unless they are already actually used by the production app.

### Analytics / technical information

Describe normal technical information that may be collected to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service, but do not invent specific analytics providers.

If the current codebase contains known analytics/telemetry SDKs, inspect the project and reflect those accurately.

### Third-party services

Include a general section explaining that Post Round relies on third-party service providers for things such as hosting, databases, authentication, AI processing, payments, analytics, or infrastructure where applicable.

Do not invent providers or claim that a provider receives data unless that is supported by the actual implementation.

### Data retention

Use careful language. Do not invent specific retention periods.

Explain that information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and otherwise fulfill legitimate business purposes, subject to applicable law.

### Account deletion

Explain that users can request/delete their account through the Post Round account deletion functionality.

Link to:

`/delete-account`

### Contact

Include:

`support@postroundcoach.com`

### Privacy Policy design

Make the page highly readable:

* Clear page title
* Last Updated date
* Table of contents if useful
* H2/H3 sections
* Comfortable reading width
* Good mobile layout
* No giant walls of text
* Existing Post Round visual language

Use the current date as the initial "Last Updated" date if appropriate.

Do not add fake legal entity names, addresses, phone numbers, or jurisdiction-specific claims that aren't known.
