# Current intended workflow

The intended workflow is:

1. A qualifying Story is created from a player's round.
2. The qualified Story appears in the Creator Dashboard.
3. Creator clicks **Generate Content**.
4. Content is generated from the Story/context.
5. Generated content is saved to `story_candidate`.
6. Generated candidates are retrieved and displayed to the creator.
7. Creator can request player approval.
8. The appropriate `story_permissions` record represents the pending approval.
9. Player receives the approval request in the existing Post Round app.
10. Player explicitly approves.
11. `story_permissions` changes to the existing `approved` state.
12. The approved Story/content becomes available for creator use/publishing.
13. Creator cannot publish/use the Story before player approval.

Generation and approval MUST remain separate.

Do not bypass player approval.
