Update the Post Round website copy and positioning to reflect the current product accurately.

## Core brand positioning

The product name is:

**POST ROUND**

Primary positioning:

**Play. Learn. Share.**

Post Round helps golfers capture their rounds while they play, understand their game afterward, and share the story of their rounds.

This is no longer just an AI golf coaching product. The social/story-sharing experience is now a **current product feature** and should be presented that way throughout the website.

---

# 1. Hero

Use:

**POST ROUND**

**Play. Learn. Share.**

Headline:

**Your golf round is more than a score.**

Supporting copy:

**Post Round helps you capture your round as you play, learn from it afterward, and share the story with the people who care.**

Do not position the product around talking to an AI coach in the car or reconstructing the round after getting home.

---

# 2. The Story of a Round

Headline:

**The story of a round**

Copy:

**The card keeps the score. You experience everything else.**

Post Round captures your round while you're playing, combining the structure of your scorecard with the moments, thoughts, decisions, and context that make every round different.

Then Post Round helps you learn from that round — and gives you a way to share the story.

---

# 3. PLAY section

Headline:

**Capture the round as it happens.**

Copy:

**Post Round is designed to capture your round while you're on the course.**

Show the two current ways to capture a round:

### Keep the score

Use the scorecard to record your round hole by hole.

### Play with Round Buddy

Talk naturally about what happened after each hole. Tell Round Buddy about your shots, misses, putts, decisions, or anything else that matters — then give it your score.

**Round Buddy keeps track of the round while you play.**

Supporting line:

**Capture the score. Capture the context. Capture the round.**

---

# 4. ROUND BUDDY section

Make this a prominent product feature.

Headline:

**Your round, in your own words.**

Copy:

**Round Buddy is your on-course playing companion.**

After each hole, tell Round Buddy naturally what happened and finish with your score. It keeps track of the round while you play, giving you a record that includes more than numbers.

Feature points:

* Remember the moments
* Keep track of your score
* Capture the human context
* Preserve the story of the round

Do not describe Round Buddy primarily as an after-round notes tool.

---

# 5. LEARN section

Headline:

**See your game more clearly.**

Copy:

**Once the round is over, Post Round turns what you captured into something useful.**

Your scorecard and round context give the AI a much richer picture of what actually happened.

Use this section to describe:

* AI coaching
* Round analysis
* Patterns and tendencies
* Practice recommendations
* Player DNA
* Learning across multiple rounds

The emphasis should be:

**Play → capture → learn**

rather than:

**Finish → get in the car → talk to your coach**

Remove references to "the drive home", "get in the car", or similar positioning.

---

# 6. PLAYER DNA

Headline:

**Your game has patterns.**

Copy:

**Player DNA builds a living picture of how you play.**

Instead of looking at one round in isolation, Post Round can help identify recurring patterns across your rounds — from scoring tendencies to the situations that consistently help or hurt your game.

Keep this positioned as a core learning/coaching feature.

---

# 7. SHARE section — IMPORTANT: THIS IS A CURRENT FEATURE

This section must NOT say that social sharing is coming soon, future functionality, planned, or "in development."

Treat sharing your round/story as a **current Post Round feature**.

Headline:

**Every round has a story worth sharing.**

Copy:

**Your score tells people what happened. Your story tells them why it mattered.**

Post Round lets you share the story of your round, giving other people more than just a final score.

The shared story can bring together the round's scorecard and meaningful moments from the round, including context captured through Round Buddy.

Use language around:

* Share your round
* Share the story behind the score
* Share meaningful moments
* Share your own words
* Give people context, not just numbers

The key concept is:

**Score + context + your own words = the story of your round**

# 8. Social / Creator Story Sharing

Present this as an existing part of the Post Round ecosystem.

Headline:

**Share your game with the people you follow.**

Copy:

**A great round isn't just something you record. It's something you can share.**

Post Round makes it possible to turn a round into a story that other people can follow and understand.

Where appropriate, describe creator/social sharing as part of the current experience.

A shared round can include:

1. The player's scorecard
2. Selected moments from the round
3. Round Buddy context
4. The player's own words

The goal is to let someone understand **the story behind the score**, rather than simply seeing a number.

Do not describe this as a future creator feature.

Do not overclaim functionality.

Only describe specific sharing mechanics that are actually implemented in the existing application.

If the current implementation supports sharing a round/story but has limitations around exactly how creators receive or view the content, describe the capability accurately without inventing UI or workflow details.

The positioning should be:

**Social story sharing is live.**

The implementation details should remain grounded in the existing codebase.

# 12. Overall narrative

The entire website should now tell one simple story:

### PLAY

Capture your round while you're playing.

### LEARN

Understand what happened and what you can improve.

### SHARE

Share the story behind the score.

The product loop is:

**Play → Learn → Share → Play again**

This should feel like the central identity of Post Round.
