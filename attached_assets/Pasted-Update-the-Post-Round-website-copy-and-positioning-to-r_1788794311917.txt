# STRATEGIC POSITIONING

The product is now branded simply as:

**Post Round**

Do not use "Post Round Coach" as the primary product name.

The core product positioning is:

# Play. Learn. Share.

Post Round is not primarily a "drive home AI coach."

The previous website positioned the product around the idea of getting in the car after a round and talking through the round with an AI coach. We are deliberately moving away from making the drive home the central product promise because voice interaction in a moving/noisy vehicle is not reliable enough to make that a core marketing claim.

The AI coaching feature remains important.

However, the broader product story is now:

### PLAY

Capture the round naturally through:

* Scorecard
* Round Buddy
* Player observations
* What actually happened on the course

### LEARN

Use AI coaching to:

* Understand the round
* Identify patterns
* Understand why strokes were lost
* Build Player DNA over time
* Improve future performance

### SHARE

Turn meaningful rounds into stories:

* Share rounds with favourite creators
* Give creators more than just a score
* Eventually allow creators to see the scorecard plus selected Round Buddy moments/transcripts
* Use the golfer's own words to provide context behind the round

The key conceptual shift is:

**The round itself is the foundation of the product.**

The scorecard tells us what happened.

The player's own words provide context.

AI helps the player understand it.

Sharing turns meaningful rounds into stories.
